Are you ready for the GENIUS Act?

The OCC's final rule implementing the Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins (GENIUS) Act sets the requirements for payment stablecoin issuers and custodians under its jurisdiction. Choose your profile, check off what you've already done, then get your readiness score.

This checklist is based on the OCC final rule announced on October 21, 2026. Bank Secrecy Act, anti-money-laundering and sanctions requirements will be set in a separate OCC rulemaking, but the Act's own requirements in those areas still apply.

Issuers supervised by other regulators (the Federal Reserve, FDIC, NCUA or a state regulator) will follow those regulators' rules. This page is not legal advice.
